## Data and formats
Odin | Its room data, quiz answers, puzzle solutions, Arrow Align layouts and three skull textures, used under its license (BSD-3-Clause); its dungeon waypoint format and its party texts for Mimic, Prince and Bat.
NoammAddons | The skull textures of the Watcher and his blood mobs (data, CC0).
NoammAddons and Odin | The rules for where an etherwarp lands.
Skyblocker | Two measured values of the Pickobulus throw.
SkyHanni-REPO | Its list of sea creatures (data, MIT).
SkyHanni | Its corpse sharing format is read and written, its party message of a rare catch is read.
## Parts and services
Hypixel API | Bazaar prices, items and the mayor.
SkyCofl (sky.coflnet.com) | Auction prices: lowest BIN, median and volume of every auction item.
NotEnoughUpdates repository | Every item and its recipes, pets' heads, sacks, enchant levels and the Abiphone contacts.
